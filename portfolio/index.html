Bio

I compose bespoke music that empowers visual storytelling from my studio in Reno, Nevada. My unconventional musical background spans Jazz piano, classical composition, Harpejji and vocal performance — providing diverse influences that enhance each project’s unique needs. After a short but successful career in civil engineering, I’ve returned to my musical roots with fresh perspective and professional discipline. I’m no stranger to hitting deadlines and working in a team to build a shared vision.

Recently graduating from Michael Whittaker's Scorecraft Academy, I built my musical foundation through a lifetime of piano lessons, San Francisco Conservatory's Pre-College classical composition program and Berklee's Five-Week Summer intensive for Jazz Piano Performance during my high school years.

Currently developing production music and original pop compositions, I'm actively seeking new collaborations with filmmakers, game developers and content creators. Please explore my latest projects and musical explorations on my blog.
